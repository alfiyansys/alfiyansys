# Hi, I'm Ian 👋

> Infrastructure & Technology Leader · Building the boring, reliable bits under the pretty UIs

I'm **Muhammad Alfiyan Syamsuddin** — VP of Technology & Infrastructure at [Trustmedis](https://trustmedis.com), where I've been designing and running the technology backbone since 2018. My work lives at the intersection of networks, servers, and security: systems that are stable, observable, and hard to break.

When I'm not leading the engineering team, I contribute to open-source infrastructure tooling, most notably [Mata Elang](https://github.com/mata-elang-stable), a distributed IDS/SIEM platform built in collaboration with PENS research institution.
